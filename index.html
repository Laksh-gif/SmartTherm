The Problem

In commercial buildings, offices, and industries, air conditioners are often used inefficiently. ACs continue running even when rooms are empty, temperatures are set unnecessarily low, and there is no proper real-time monitoring of electricity usage. This leads to excessive power consumption, higher operational costs, overload on electrical systems, and unnecessary energy wastage.

Proposed Solution

SmartTherm is a smart cooling management system that combines hardware sensors, ESP32 controllers, and monitoring software to automatically optimize AC usage based on room conditions and occupancy.

The system detects whether people are present in a room, monitors temperature and energy usage, and then automatically adjusts cooling to reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.

Project Objectives

  • Reducing AC wastage across large scale deployments
  • Lowering electricity consumption significantly
  • Improving overall energy efficiency & sustainability
  • Reducing overload stress on main power grids
  • Creating smarter, automated building management
