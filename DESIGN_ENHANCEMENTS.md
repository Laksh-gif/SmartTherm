## Animation & Transition Effects

### Keyframe Animations
- **float** / **floatReverse**: 20-25s smooth floating background orbs
- **fadeIn**: 0.6s smooth fade with slight slide up
- **titleGlow**: 3s pulsing glow effect on main title
- **fadeInUp**: Subtitle fade-in with upward movement
- **badge-pulse**: 2s pulsing notification badge
- **icon-bounce**: 0.6s bouncy icon entrance
- **shimmer**: Gradient sweep effect on buttons
- **scale-in**: Elements scale up from 0.95 to 1

### Transition Timings
- **Standard**: 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.8, 0.25, 1)
- **Smooth**: 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) - bouncy easing
- **Fast**: Quick responsive feedback

## Performance Optimizations
- Efficient use of `::before` and `::after` pseudo-elements for effects
- Hardware-accelerated transforms (translate, scale)
- Optimized animations with will-change (where needed)
- Smooth 60fps animations without blocking interactions
